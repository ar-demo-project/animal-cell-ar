Ready to explore AR?

This app uses your camera to recognize three marker patterns and place a different 3D object on each one.

  1. Open this page using HTTPS or localhost.
  2. Tap Start AR Camera and allow camera access.
  3. Point the camera at a supported marker.
  4. Choose a model to control its rotation.

Scanning guide

  • Robot: standard Hiro marker.
  • Mecha: standard Kanji marker.
  • Board: Letter A pattern marker (external AR.js asset).
  • Keep the entire marker visible, flat, well-lit, and in focus.
  • If no object appears, check camera permission and internet, then reload.
